Verify setup
==============

.. only:: appliance
   
   The Cloud AI SDKs and Cloud AI 100 inference cards are preloaded and preinstalled in commercially available AI On-Prem Appliances. As part of setting up the appliance box, you should work with your system administrators to ensure that the Cloud AI SDKs and AI 100 cards are installed properly. See `Appliance setup overview <https://docs.qualcomm.com/doc/80-92111-1/topic/appliance-setup-overview.html>`_ for information on how to verify the appliance box setup. 
   
   The following Cloud AI SDK installation instructions are provided here are for reference purposes.
